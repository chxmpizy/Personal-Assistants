# Pick one critical-but-simple pain and ship a solo SaaS in 30 days

**Created:** 2026-10-21
**Status:** draft

## One-line summary
Find a critical, simple, prosumer/B2B pain where a single AI-API pipeline (one input → one output) can be built solo in ~30 days and sell at ~$29/mo from day one.

## The itch
The user wants to copy the shape of the "long video → 10 shorts" success story: a problem that is (a) critical enough that people already pay a human or waste real hours on it, (b) simple enough that the MVP is a straight pipeline, and (c) small enough that one person can ship it in a month. They don't yet know *which* pain; they want a researched pick plus a build plan.

## Core question(s)
1. Which 5–8 pain points fit the "critical + simple + 1-month-solo-buildable + $29–$49/mo monetizable" shape, and which has the clearest build-in-public lane for an IG-first Thai founder?
2. For the top pick: what's the exact stack, 4-week milestone plan, first-100-users distribution, and the single riskiest assumption to kill in week 1?

## Known constraints / context
- **Solo founder.** No co-founder, no team to pay. (inferred)
- **~30-day build window.** Must ship a chargeable product in a month, not a prototype.
- **AI-wrapper is fine.** The "wow" comes from the pipeline + packaging, not from custom ML.
- **Audience leverage:** user runs IG @champ.ratt and GitHub `chxmpizy/Personal-Assistants` and is comfortable building in public. (from memory)
- **Thai founder, English-first markets preferred for monetization,** but a Thai-market play should be surfaced if obvious. (inferred)
- **Price anchor: $29/mo** — the median per-tool spend for solopreneurs in 2026 per market data, so pricing should cluster there.
- **No network-effect, no marketplace, no hardware, no team seats day one.** Must be 1 input → 1 output.

## Success looks like
A research report (`research/2026-10-21-solo-startup-1-month-pains.md`) with:
- 5–8 ranked candidate pains with competitor pricing and audience evidence
- A top-3 shortlist with reasoning
- A single `#1` pick plus concrete stack, weekly milestones, first-100-users plan, and the week-1 kill test

## Suggested next step
Pass to **Research** with focus on: competitor pricing in short-form video / UGC ads / Reddit lead-gen / receipt OCR / dunning spaces, and which has the biggest **price gap** below venture-funded incumbents for a solo $29/mo play.

## Open questions for the user
- Any domain you want to **exclude** (e.g. "I don't want to touch e-commerce" or "no video — too crowded")?
- Comfort level with **payments / Stripe + fintech-adjacent** niches (dunning, invoicing) — these are the highest-margin per market data but boring to build-in-public around.
- Interest in a **Thai-market variant** (Lazada/Shopee sellers, Thai creators) vs. English-only?
