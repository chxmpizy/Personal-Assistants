# Build a personal brand across Business, Tech & Self-growth on Instagram and TikTok

**Created:** 2026-10-20
**Status:** draft

## One-line summary

Design and launch a personal brand on Instagram and TikTok that credibly covers three themes — Business, Tech, and Self-growth — without diluting any of them.

## The itch

The user wants to build an owned audience and a public identity around topics they clearly already care about. They're asking "how do I approach this *system*" — meaning they want a repeatable method (positioning, pillars, cadence, platform mechanics), not just a list of video ideas. The real question underneath: *can I run three topics at once, or do I need to narrow?*

## Core question(s)

1. **Positioning** — How should these three topics be unified into a single, memorable brand angle (so the account doesn't feel like three unrelated feeds)?
2. **Content system** — What is a sustainable weekly content system across IG + TikTok (pillars, formats, cadence, repurposing) for a solo creator in 2026?
3. **Platform mechanics** — What currently works on IG and TikTok specifically for Business / Tech / Self-growth niches (hooks, formats, lengths, hashtags vs. SEO, Reels vs. carousels, etc.)?

## Known constraints / context

- Solo creator, two platforms simultaneously (IG + TikTok). *(inferred)*
- Three topics is unusually broad; most "niche advice" says pick one. The user wants an approach that *honors* the breadth. *(inferred)*
- English-speaking audience assumed. *(inferred — open question)*
- Budget, available weekly hours, and current audience size unknown. *(open question)*
- No stated monetization goal yet — audience-first vs. product-first path is undecided. *(open question)*

## Success looks like

A short, actionable playbook the user can execute starting this week:

- A one-sentence brand positioning statement that ties the three topics together.
- A content pillar system (3–5 pillars) and a weekly posting cadence per platform.
- Platform-specific mechanics for 2026 (what formats, hook styles, and growth loops are working now).
- A 30-day starter plan so the system is testable, not just theoretical.
