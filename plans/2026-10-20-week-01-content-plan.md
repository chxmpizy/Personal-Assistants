## 2026 platform-mechanic reminders (baked in from research)

- Keyword in **first spoken sentence** AND **first line of caption** — primary SEO lever. Hashtags are secondary.
- On-screen text version of the hook on every video, 6–8 words max.
- 60–90s is the sweet spot for both IG and TikTok 2026.
- Share-prompt > follow-prompt on Reel/TikTok closes (sends weighted 3–5× likes).
- Comment-prompt > share-prompt in **carousel captions** (comments outweigh sends on carousels).
- Clean master export always — never post from a TikTok download to IG (4-signal detector will throttle).
- Reply within 2 hours of posting to boost comment ranking.
