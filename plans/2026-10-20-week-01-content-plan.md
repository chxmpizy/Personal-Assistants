## 2026 platform-mechanic reminders baked in (pulled from research/2026-10-20)

- **Keyword in first spoken sentence AND first line of caption.** This is the SEO lever. Hashtags are secondary now.
- **On-screen text version of the hook on every video.** 6–8 words max.
- **60–90s is the sweet spot** for both IG and TikTok 2026 (don't go longer unless the piece earns it).
- **Share-prompt > follow-prompt** on Reel/TikTok closes (sends weighted 3–5× likes).
- **Comment-prompt > share-prompt** on carousel captions (comments outweigh sends on carousels specifically).
- **Clean master export** — never post from a TikTok download to IG; the 4-signal detector will throttle you.
- **Reply within 2 hours** of posting to boost comment ranking.
