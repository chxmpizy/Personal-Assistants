# Script — Stop reading business books

**Platform(s):** IG Reel + TikTok
**Pillar:** Provoke · Business
**Topic cluster:** Consumption-vs-action trap for entrepreneurs
**Target length:** 20–40s (optimized for watch-time completion %)
**Keyword to lead with:** stop reading business books

## Shot list

| # | Time | Visual | Spoken (VO) | On-screen text |
|---|------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | 0–3s | Me holding a famous business book, chuck it off-screen, deadpan stare, no smile. Tight MCU. | "Stop reading business books. You already know the answer — you just don't want to do it." | The book isn't the problem. |
| 2 | 3–8s | Pan across my desk: 6 books stacked, half-read, Post-its everywhere, laptop open to a blank Notion page. | "You've read 'Lean Startup' three times. You still haven't talked to a customer." | Pattern: you consume to feel productive. |
| 3 | 8–18s | Me pointing at the camera, then at the stack. Hard cut to me closing the top book. | "Pick any problem in your business. You already know the next move. You can say it out loud right now." | You already know. Say it out loud. |
| 4 | 18–30s | Me sitting still, direct eye contact, no cuts for 4 seconds. Then subtle lean-in. | "The block isn't knowledge. It's that doing it means you might fail — and then you can't blame the book anymore." | Real block: fear of being seen trying. |
| 5 | 30–40s | Me sliding the book stack into a drawer, closing it. Smash cut to black with text. | "Close the book. Send this to the one who needs it. Then go do the thing." | **Tag the friend you just thought of.** |

**VO word count:** 78 words. Hook pays off at 0:08 (within 10s). Every beat has on-screen text.

## Caption (platform-specific)

**TikTok caption:**
Stop reading business books — you already know the answer, you just don't want to do it. Name the one move you've been avoiding. Tag the friend who stacks books like trophies. #entrepreneur #startup #actionoverinformation #builder #provoke

**IG caption:**
Stop reading business books. You already know the answer — you just don't want to do it.

The reading isn't research anymore. It's a costume. It lets you feel like a founder without ever being seen trying.

The uncomfortable part: pick any stuck problem in your business right now. You can say the next move out loud. You already know.

The block isn't knowledge. It's that doing it means it might not work — and then the book can't carry the blame for you.

Close the book. Do the thing. Tag the one person who needs to see this today.

— champ

## Production notes
- B-roll needed: close-up of book stack with Post-its; drawer closing on books; blank Notion page cursor blinking
- Props: 1 recognizable business book (Lean Startup / Atomic Habits / E-Myth), stack of 5–6 more, Post-it notes, laptop
- Framing: tight MCU for all talking shots — no wides. Hard eye contact.
- Audio: dry room tone, no music under VO. Optional subtle low drone from 18s–30s for the "mirror" beat.
- Pacing: hard cuts only, no transitions. Shot 4 holds longer than feels comfortable — that's the point.
- Captions: burn in on-screen text manually, not auto-caption. Bold sans-serif, high contrast, centered.
- Clean-master export: YES (no watermark, 1080x1920, H.264, 60fps source → 30fps delivery)
