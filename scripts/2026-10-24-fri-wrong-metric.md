# Script — What I learned losing a year to the wrong metric

**Platform(s):** TikTok (primary), Instagram Reel (repurpose)
**Pillar:** Personal · Self-growth
**Topic cluster:** Builder lessons / dashboards & decisions
**Target length:** 60–120s (longer-form, story arc)
**Keyword to lead with:** wrong metric (secondary: vanity metric trap)

## Shot list

| # | Time | Visual | Spoken (VO) | On-screen text |
|---|------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | 0–3s | Split-screen — LEFT: smiling screenshot of a hockey-stick graph. RIGHT: a bank balance quietly ticking down. Both timestamps read "Day 1." | "365 days ago I was 'winning' on every chart that mattered — and quietly going broke behind the scenes. The number I was chasing had a twin, and I was watching the wrong one." | **Winning the chart. Losing the year.** |
| 2 | 3–15s | Me at a desk at night, laptop glow, pulling up a dashboard, nodding, then slowly stopping. Soft "oh no" micro-expression. | "I was a builder deep in 'busy but not winning.' Every Monday I'd open the dashboard, see the line going up, and tell myself the plan was working. For twelve months straight." | Busy. Not winning. |
| 3 | 15–30s | Zoom into the dashboard: a big bold number labeled "SIGNUPS" with a green arrow. Confetti emoji floats across the screen, I swat it away. | "The metric I was chasing was signups. It felt right — it's up and to the right, it looks like growth, and every tweet about it got love. So I optimized the whole year around it. Landing pages, ads, launches, threads. All for that one number." | The wrong metric: SIGNUPS ↑ |
| 4 | 30–50s | Dashboard pulls back to reveal a SECOND number next to it: "Weekly Active Users" — flat line. Then "Paid Retention: 11%". My face drops. | "Here's the twin I wasn't watching: weekly active users. Signups is the number you brag about. WAU is the number that pays rent. One is a mirror. The other is a signal. I spent a year polishing the mirror while the signal flatlined." | The twin metric: WAU (flat) |
| 5 | 50–75s | Me holding up three fingers to camera. Each question appears as a bold card that stamps onto screen. Quick cuts between my face and the cards. | "So before you touch your dashboard today, run the 3-question test. One: if this number doubled tomorrow, would my bank account feel it in 30 days? Two: am I tracking the thing I DO, or the thing that actually changes a user's life? Three: what's the twin — the quiet metric that would catch me lying to myself?" | 3-question test: 1. Would it hit the bank in 30d? / 2. Doing vs. serving? / 3. What's the twin? |
| 6 | 75–100s | Me at the desk again, calmer. I drag the "SIGNUPS" card off the dashboard, pin "WAU" and "Paid Retention" to the top. Small checkmark animates. | "The day I swapped the top of my dashboard, the actual work changed. I stopped shipping landing pages and started fixing the second week. Revenue didn't double overnight — but for the first time in a year, the line that mattered started moving. That's the whole lesson. Your dashboard is a bet on what matters. Make sure you're betting on the right twin." | Your dashboard is a bet. |
| 7 | 100–120s | Camera close, direct eye contact. Soft smile. End card appears: handle + "tag the friend." | "Tag the friend who's 'crushing it' on paper but keeps telling you they're stuck. Odds are they're winning a chart and losing a year — and this is the nudge." | Tag the friend "crushing it" but secretly stuck. @champ.ratt |

## Production notes
- B-roll needed: dashboard screen recording (fake/anonymized OK) with SIGNUPS big + WAU small, then the swap; bank balance ticker (stylized, no real numbers); night-desk ambient shot; close-up of hand dragging cards on screen.
- Props: laptop, desk lamp (warm), notebook (open to a page with "SIGNUPS" crossed out and "WAU" written below — one quick cutaway).
- On-screen text style: bold sans-serif, white with black stroke, left-aligned top-third; "twin metric" moments get a subtle highlight swipe.
- Audio: no trending music under VO — keep it dry so the 3-question test lands. Light ambient pad under beats 4–6 only.
- Pacing: cut on the "twin" reveal (beat 4) and on each of the three questions (beat 5). Hold the final frame 1.5s before end card.
- Clean-master export: YES (1080x1920, no captions burned-in for the master; separate burned version for TikTok).
